# Teaching moves — the one yardstick (David 2026-11-04: "the goal is TEACH LIKE HE DOES")

One code per teaching move a coach makes while thinking out loud. A line gets
1–4 codes: every teaching move it actually MAKES, not every topic it touches.
Use only these codes. A line that only narrates the moves ("knight f3, knight
c6") or chats (greetings, the stream, the clock talk with no lesson) is
X-NARRATION.

## The move and its purpose
- M-PURPOSE-QUIET — what a quiet move (no capture, no check) is FOR: the job it does, the square it prepares.
- M-PURPOSE-PREVENT — what a move stops: the threat or idea it takes away.
- M-OPP-PURPOSE — what THEIR move is for: their idea, their threat, their plan behind it.
- M-TWO-JOBS — one move doing two things at once (dual purpose).
- M-VERDICT — a judgement of the move just played: good, a mistake, the best, inaccurate, why.
- M-RECAPTURE — which piece recaptures, and why that one.
- M-TRADE — a trade decision: whether to trade, which piece to trade, what the trade gives or gives up.
- M-TEMPO — gaining or losing time: a move with tempo, a wasted move, a piece moved twice.
- M-REFUTED — the tempting / natural move ruled out WITH its refutation ("if you take, then …").
- M-COMMON-WRONG — what most players play here and why it is wrong.
- M-OPP-COST — what the move gives up (the cost, the concession).
- M-REPLY-TREE — "if they play X, I play Y": their likely replies, each with its answer.
- M-PROVOKE — a move that asks them to commit, provokes a weakness or a decision.
- M-MOVE-ORDER — the order of moves matters here (this first, then that), outside opening theory.

## Thinking (the method)
- H-CANDIDATES — lists the moves he considers here.
- H-TWO-GOOD — two good moves compared: why one over the other.
- H-MENU — the options at a branch laid out (the menu: A, B or C).
- H-CALC — calculating out loud, visualising a line ("I go here, he goes there…").
- H-EVAL — an overall assessment of the position: who is better and why (imbalances, compensation).
- H-PRACTICAL — practical play: the clock, the opponent's level, what is hard to face, a swindle.
- H-DONT-PANIC — keep calm, the position is not as bad as it looks / a false alarm.
- H-OPP-THINK — asks what the opponent wants / puts himself in their shoes.
- H-HABIT — a method or habit to carry into other games ("always ask…", "before every move…").
- H-WISHLIST — what he would like to achieve if he had a free move.

## Tactics and danger
- T-TACTIC — a named tactic on the board: fork, pin, skewer, discovered attack, deflection…
- T-THREAT — a threat (theirs or his), named.
- T-HANG — a loose / hanging / undefended piece.
- T-MATE — a mating pattern or mating attack.
- T-LINE — a concrete line played out in words (three or more moves).
- T-HIDDEN — a hidden idea or resource, one move deeper than it looks.
- T-CHECKFIRST — checks, captures, threats: the forcing moves first.
- T-TRAP-AHEAD — a trap named before it lands (theirs to avoid, or one to set).
- T-COUNT — counting: attackers and defenders, a race, tempi, pawns to promotion.
- T-SAC-INITIATIVE — a sacrifice for the initiative or the attack.
- T-INDIRECT-DEFENCE — defending indirectly (a counter-attack, an in-between move) instead of directly.
- T-FALSE-ALARM — something that looks dangerous but is not, and why.

## Strategy
- S-PLAN — a plan or a target: what to do over the next several moves.
- S-PLAN-VS-PLAN — both sides' plans set against each other.
- S-MANEUVER — a piece manoeuvre: a route over several moves.
- S-SQUARE-CHOICE — which square for a piece, and why this one over the others.
- S-BREAK — a pawn break.
- S-SPACE — space.
- S-KING-ATTACK — attacking (or defending) the king.
- S-CASTLING — the castling decision: when, which side, or castling by hand.
- S-PROPHYLAXIS — prophylaxis against a PLAN (not a one-move threat).
- S-TENSION — holding or releasing tension (pawn tension, piece tension).
- S-TIMING — the timing of a plan: now, not yet, first this.
- S-TRIGGER — the missing ingredient / the trigger: "once X happens, Y works".
- S-LONG-HORIZON — a long-term consequence of a decision (a structure that will matter many moves later).
- S-WHEN-AHEAD — how to play when winning (simplify, stop counterplay).
- S-PUSH-OR-HOLD — push a pawn now, or keep it back.

## The position
- P-STRUCTURE — pawn structure: weaknesses, holes, outposts, doubled / isolated pawns, open / closed.
- P-PIECE-QUALITY — a good or bad piece, an active or passive piece.
- P-PRINCIPLE — a general principle (development, the centre, the open file…).
- P-OPENING-IDEA — the idea of the opening: what this opening is about, its typical plans.
- P-MOVE-ORDER — opening move order or a transposition.
- P-THEORY — theory, repertoire, history of the line (what the books / engines say, who played it).
- P-EXCEPTION — the exception to a principle, and why it applies here.

## The endgame
- E-CONVERT — converting an advantage.
- E-PASSER — a passed pawn.
- E-TECHNIQUE — an endgame technique (opposition, the king's activity, the rook behind the passer…).

## Not teaching
- X-NARRATION — only says the moves, or talk that teaches nothing.
