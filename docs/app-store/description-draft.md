**Chess Academy Pro — the coach that LEARNS HOW YOU PLAY.**

Every other chess app gives you the same lessons as everyone else. This one watches YOUR games, learns what you know and what you don't, and changes what it teaches you next. It's the difference between a textbook and a coach.

**A COACH THAT ACTUALLY TEACHES**
Play a real game and your coach talks you through it like a strong player sitting beside you:
• "The move is c5 — it hits the centre before their knight closes the door."
• "Their bishop pins your knight to your queen. The knight can't move without losing the queen, so they'll pile on it."
• "Taking looks natural, but count first: you take, they take back, and you're a pawn down."
Every word is worked out from the position on the board, never guessed. Arrows show the move and the whole line behind it.

**IT LEARNS HOW YOU PLAY**
Your coach keeps a map of your chess. Ideas you've never faced, it teaches fully. Mistakes you repeat, it catches every time until they stop. Skills you've proven on your own, it stops explaining. A beginner hears everything; a strong player hears only what's new. No two players get the same coach.

**TRAPS: WARNED, WALKED THROUGH, THEN TESTED**
Your coach knows the traps real club players fall into, and how often they fall:
• Italian Game: after d4, about 3% of club players grab the pawn with …Nxd4. Your coach shows why Bxf7+ followed by Nxe5+ tears Black's position apart.
• Scotch Game: trading knights on d4 looks natural, and 1 in 4 club players does it. Your coach shows how the queen takes back and grabs the centre.
The first time you meet a trap, it warns you and plays out the whole punishment on the board. Once you've dodged it, it goes quiet the next time to see if you spot it yourself. Pass, and it trusts you. Fall in, and it teaches you again. Real learning, not memorizing.

**PLAY ANY OPENING YOU ASK**
Say "play the Caro-Kann" or "let's do the King's Gambit" and your coach plays it with you, explaining what the opening is built to do, what it provokes, and how sharp it is.

**GUIDED WALKTHROUGHS — YOUR OPENING VS THEIRS**
Pick two openings, like the King's Indian Attack against the French, and watch them meet in a guided, narrated game. You'll see both plans unfold move by move and learn how to fight the setup you'll actually face.

**A DEEP OPENING LIBRARY**
Over 3,000 named openings and variations, built from real master games. Openings come with their sublines and key tabiya positions, middlegame plans, the model games that made them famous, and the traps to use and avoid, each taught on a Watch → Learn → Practice → Play ladder.

**AN OPPONENT YOUR STRENGTH FROM MOVE ONE**
No rating quiz, no setup. Your opponent adjusts to you as you play, from your very first game.

**REVIEW THAT EXPLAINS YOUR GAME**
Your coach walks you through the moments that decided your game: where it turned, what you missed, and what to look for next time.

**DRILLS BUILT FROM YOUR MISTAKES**
Import your games and every tactic you missed becomes a puzzle, made from your own chess.

Chess Academy Pro isn't a puzzle app with a chatbot bolted on. It's a coach that knows your game — and gets better at teaching you every time you play.
