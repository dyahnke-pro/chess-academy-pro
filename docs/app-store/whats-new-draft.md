**A coach that learns you.**

Most chess apps give every player the same lessons. This one watches how you actually play — and what it learns changes what it says next.

**Learn with Coach — a game that talks back.** Play a real game while your coach talks you through it like a strong player sitting beside you. It names your opening and what it's trying to do, tells you why a move works, what your opponent is threatening, and which natural-looking move walks into trouble. Every fact comes from the position on the board, with arrows showing the move and the whole line behind it.

**It knows what you need to hear — and what you don't.** Your coach keeps a map of your game. Ideas you've never faced, it teaches fully. Mistakes you keep repeating, it points out every time until they stop. Skills you've proven on your own, it stops explaining — so your time goes where you need it. A beginner hears everything; a strong player hears only what's new.

**Traps: warned, then tested.** Your coach knows the traps club players fall into. The first time you meet one, it warns you and shows the punishment. Once you've dodged it with the warning, it goes quiet the next time to see if you spot it yourself. Pass, and it trusts you. Fall in, and it teaches you again. Real learning, not memorizing.

**An opponent your strength from the first move.** No rating quiz, no setup — the opponent adjusts as you play, from your first game.

**Play — just you and the board.** No interruptions. The coach speaks only when you ask.

**Review — your game, explained.** Your coach walks you through the moments that decided the game: where it turned, what you missed, and what to look for next time.

**Openings — know why, not just what.** Every opening now comes with its identity: what it provokes, how sharp it is, and the master games that made it famous.

**Tactics & Weaknesses — built from your own games.** Your drills come from your own mistakes, so every puzzle is one you've actually faced.
