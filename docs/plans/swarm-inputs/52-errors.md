# The 52 errors from the Learn free-play + Review walk (2026-10-06)
Tapes: learn5.log (Learn games L1, L2 as Black), rev-g1.tape, rev-g2.tape (their reviews).

Every error I found on the four tapes: two Learn games (L1 = game 1, L2 = game 2) and their two reviews (R1, R2). Each is listed once, grouped by kind. Those marked **(check)** look wrong but need confirming on the board before I'd call them false.

**A. False or doubtful claims**
1. L2: "knight to g4 was the move — it would swing pieces toward their king". The real reason is that your knight on f6 was attacked by the g5 pawn.
2. L2: "your knight takes on d4 and is taken on the spot". It wasn't taken in the game; the f6 knight was.
3. L2: "f5 was the move — the idea is to create a passed pawn on e6" **(check)**.
4. L2: "Their rook to b1 prepares queen to b5, to take the half-open b-file". A queen on b5 doesn't "take the file" **(check)**.
5. L2: "Their king to f1 prepares queen to e1, to take the half-open e-file" **(check)**.
6. R1: "rook takes e8… a move earlier, their bishop would have taken on e8 and won your rook". The bishop wasn't on e8 a move earlier.
7. R1: "your answer was d4" said on the opponent's move, as if you played d4. You played Nxg4.
8. R1: "your answer was d4" said again on a later opponent move. Same template, wrong position.
9. R1: "the stronger move was d4" on a third student move **(check)**.
10. R2: "clean chess on both sides, decided by one moment: knight to c6". You lost several pieces.
11. R2: "controlling the only open file is what decided it". You lost your queen.
12. R1: "That left your knight hanging" at the castling move. The piece actually lost was the bishop.
