## The honest verdict
All 10 are **defensible recommendations** under the win% standard: each wins ≥45%
at the buyer's level. Where eval and club results disagree, **club results are the
truth for the buyer** — these are aggressive/surprise weapons that score, which is
exactly what we're selling. The gambits (Stafford, Hamppe-Muzio) are labeled
surprise weapons, never "sound mainlines." The only line worth a second look is
#5's deep Bxf7 sub-line (−2.09); the rest are clean keeps.

**Contrast:** the 7 we're FIXING fail at BOTH levels (bad eval AND <45% club) —
that's the line between "ugly but wins" (keep) and "just bad" (fix/cut).
