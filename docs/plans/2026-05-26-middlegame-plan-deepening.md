## Goal
Take every masterclass opening's middlegame plans from "states the idea" to
"plays the full plan out" — longer lines that actually EXECUTE the pawn break /
maneuver / attack to its payoff, with rich two-register narration and
lead-the-eye arrows on every beat. Grounded, never invented.

## Grounding rails (G3 — non-negotiable)
- **Moves come from real sources, verified by chess.js.** Extend lines with the
  most-played continuations from the local masters DB
  (`public/data/openings-masters-db.json` → `positions[<4-field FEN>]` =
  `[{san, games, rating}]`), or the canonical `openings-lichess.json` lines.
  Never hand-invent a move.
- **Ideas come from the books + the plan's own declared themes**, recorded in
  `sources[]` (`book:<id>` | `concept:<id>` | reputable URL). Book corpus
  (`chess-concepts.json`, `opening-book-pages.json`) covers classical openings;
  modern openings ground on universal principles + reputable online refs.
- **Board-facts are gated.** Arrows/highlights regenerated by
  `scripts/add-leadeye-to-plans.mjs` (sight-line verified; every endpoint must
  appear as a square token in that move's annotation). `narrationAccuracy` /
  `middlegamePlanner.test` enforce.
- **When unsure: leave blank / skip / ASK — never guess.**

## Expansion pipeline (repeatable, grounded)
1. Replay `line.fen` + `line.moves` with chess.js → end position.
2. `positions[<4-field FEN>]` in masters DB → ranked continuations; pick the
   one that advances the plan's `pawnBreaks`/`pieceManeuvers` goal square.
3. Walk N plies (chess.js-verified each step); stop at the payoff square.
4. Author per-new-move `annotations` (full) + `learnCues` (≤8-word short),
   grounded in the plan's themes + book corpus; add/keep `sources[]`.
5. Re-run leadeye; run gates.
