# Convert: trade pieces, keep pawns, use the king

## Principle
Material won by force is the clearest advantage: trade pieces, not pawns, and in the endgame convert with the king and the passed pawn, not with tricks; avoid reducing to opposite-coloured bishops.

## When it applies / exceptions
See recovery/up-material.md and recovery/better-drawish-endgame.md.

## Deterministic check
- Ledger d ≥ +1 at the quiet end; endgame conversion: student passer + king distance.

## Phrasings
1. You won material by force; now trade pieces and keep pawns.
2. In the ending, convert with the king and the passed pawn.
3. Ahead, keep it simple: swap down, no tricks.
4. Don't trade into opposite bishops; that's where wins go to draw.
5. An advantage you can't convert is just a draw.
