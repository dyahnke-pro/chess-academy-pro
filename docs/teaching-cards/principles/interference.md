# Interference

## Principle
Dropping a piece between a defender and what it guards cuts the line, and the target hangs.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat.

## Deterministic check
- A line defender D guards target T along a ray; a legal move of the attacking side lands on a square strictly between D and T (SEE on that square may be negative: the point is the cut), after which T's defenders drop to 0 and T is attacked.

## Phrasings
1. A piece dropped in between cut your guard's line.
2. Block the line their defender guards along, and the target hangs.
3. Every guard runs along a line; watch what can block it.
4. When they step in between, your pieces stop helping each other.
5. Cut the line between their pieces.
