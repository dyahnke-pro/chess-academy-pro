# The obvious move: check their best reply

## Principle
The move that jumps out first is often the one they're ready for; before playing it, find the piece that answers it. A move that must be taken back is two moves wasted.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat.

## Deterministic check
- pictureVerdict T: the try's `eye` rank ≤ 3 and its refutation exists (reply, replier); grouping by replier square.

## Phrasings
1. The move that jumps out is often the one they're ready for.
2. Before the obvious move, ask what their best reply is.
3. Find the piece that answers your idea; that's their defender.
4. A move you have to take back wastes two moves.
5. Check what a move allows, not just what it attacks.
