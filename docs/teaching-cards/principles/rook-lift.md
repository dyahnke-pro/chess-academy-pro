# The rook lift

## Principle
When the files are closed, a rook can climb up the board and swing across the third rank to join an attack on the king.

## When it applies / exceptions
Middlegame, closed or semi-closed centre.

## Deterministic check
- named manoeuvre (pictureVerdict P `rook lift`): rook moves up its file to the 3rd/4th rank then laterally toward the enemy king's wing, the rank path clear.

## Phrasings
1. The files are closed, so lift the rook and swing it across.
2. A rook can climb the board and join the attack.
3. Their rook came up the board and over to your king.
4. Bring the rook up the third rank toward their king.
5. No open file? Make the rook travel along a rank instead.
