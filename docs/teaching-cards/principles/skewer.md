# Skewers

## Principle
A skewer attacks a valuable piece with a weaker one behind it on the same line; the front piece must move and the one behind falls.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat. Line pieces only.

## Deterministic check
- Ray from an enemy/student bishop/rook/queen: first piece hit is worth MORE than the second (or is the king), second piece undefended or worth more than the attacker, and the front piece's flight leaves the ray (all legal king/front moves keep the back piece attacked).

## Phrasings
1. Their king had to move, and the rook behind it fell.
2. Two pieces on one line with the big one in front is a skewer.
3. Keep your king and rook off the same open line.
4. The front piece must step aside, and the back one goes.
5. A check down that line hits what stands behind.
