# Giving up a bishop gives up its colour

## Principle
Trade your last bishop of one colour and its squares belong to their bishop of that colour; repair with pawns on that colour and a knight on the holes.

## When it applies / exceptions
See recovery/colour-conceded.md for the full check and repair.

## Deterministic check
- Bishop-colour census: student has 0 bishops on colour C, opponent ≥1; targets = student pawns/holes on C attacked by that bishop.

## Phrasings
1. Trade that bishop and you give away its colour.
2. Without your dark bishop, their dark bishop rules those squares.
3. Before you trade a bishop, ask which squares you're giving away.
4. Your pawns on that colour are its targets now.
5. Plant a piece on their weak colour; they can't chase it.
