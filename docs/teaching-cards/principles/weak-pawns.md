# Weak pawns: isolated, backward, doubled

## Principle
A pawn no pawn can guard needs a piece to babysit it, and the square in front becomes a home for their knight. Against it: blockade, then pile up; the ending is where it falls. With it: keep pieces active, don't tie them down. A weak pawn only matters if the opponent can reach it.

## When it applies / exceptions
IQP seat split: the owner keeps pieces and attacks (middlegame); the opponent trades and blockades. Doubled pawns are weak mainly when also isolated. Overstated: 'weak pawns are a small problem in the middlegame' — a target on a half-open file can lose in the middlegame; say 'a bigger problem in the endgame'.

## Phrasings
1. A pawn no other pawn can guard needs a piece to watch it.
2. The square in front of that pawn is a home for their knight.
3. With an isolated pawn, keep the pieces on and attack.
4. Against an isolated pawn, trade pieces and block the square in front.
5. A weak pawn only matters if they can get at it.
