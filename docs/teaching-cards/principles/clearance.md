# Clearance

## Principle
Sometimes your own piece blocks a better one: move it with a threat (even as a sacrifice) and the line or square opens for the piece behind.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat.

## Deterministic check
- A friendly piece F stands on a ray (or square) that a stronger friendly piece S needs for a threat; the stored winning line's first move moves F off it WITH tempo (check/capture/threat), and S's next move gains ≥ 100cp at the quiet end.

## Phrasings
1. Your own piece was in the way of a better one.
2. Move the blocker with a threat, and the line opens.
3. They moved one piece aside to open the line behind it.
4. Ask which of your pieces is blocking a stronger one.
5. Clear the square, and the piece behind strikes.
