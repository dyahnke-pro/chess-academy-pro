# Stop their play first

## Principle
Before you press, find their one source of counterplay and take it away; a won position is lost by ignoring the counterattack. Ask every move what they want next.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat. Caveat: a 'preventive' move against nothing is a wasted tempo (Wikipedia, Initiative).

## Deterministic check
- pictureVerdict L counterplay alive (computed): name its piece/break; a student move covering the break square or trading the counterplay piece exists in the engine top-3.

## Phrasings
1. Before you press for more, stop their counterplay.
2. Ask what they want next, and take it away.
3. A won game is lost by ignoring their counterattack.
4. Their only play is that break; cover the square first.
5. With no counterplay to fear, you can take your time.
