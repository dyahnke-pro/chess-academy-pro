# The bishop pair wants open lines

## Principle
Two bishops cover both colours and are strongest in an open position; open lines for them. Against the pair, keep it closed and trade one off.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat. Weaker when pawns are on one wing only.

## Deterministic check
- Pair = two bishops on opposite colours vs not; openness = count of open/half-open files + number of central pawns (≤2 central pawns = open).

## Phrasings
1. Two bishops love open lines; open the position for them.
2. Giving up a bishop gives up the pair.
3. Against the bishop pair, keep the pawns locked.
4. With both bishops, a small edge grinds on in the ending.
5. Trade one of their bishops and the pair is gone.
