# Every defender has a job (deflection, overload, removing the guard)

## Principle
A piece that guards something has a job; move it, lure it away, give it two jobs, or remove it, and what it guarded falls.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat. Pilot row 'when one piece refutes all your tries, that piece is the real target' is the same principle (attack the defender).

## Deterministic check
- Guard map before/after: the student's moved piece was the ONLY defender of a piece/square that the opponent's best line then wins (abandoned-defender).
- Overload: one enemy piece is the sole defender of ≥2 targets attacked by the student.
- Deflection/removal: the stored winning line's first move captures or forces away a sole defender (attack it, or offer a piece it must take).

## Phrasings
1. That piece was guarding something, and when it moved, it fell.
2. Know what a piece guards before you move it.
3. Their defender had two jobs, so it couldn't do both.
4. Pull the guard away and what it guarded falls.
5. Take out the defender, and the target is loose.
