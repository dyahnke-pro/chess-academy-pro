# Discovered attacks

## Principle
Moving one piece can unmask another behind it, so two threats arrive at once; with a check, the other threat can't be answered. Watch the line behind a piece that is about to move.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat. A double check forces a king move.

## Deterministic check
- Battery: a moving piece M and a line piece L of the same side on one ray toward an enemy target T, M between them. The discovered move = any legal move of M off the ray; strong when M's own move also threatens (check, capture, attack ≥ value) or when T is the king.
- Allowed: the opponent has such a battery after the student's move and the PV uses it.

## Phrasings
1. Their piece stepped aside, and the one behind it struck.
2. Watch the piece behind: when the front one moves, it attacks.
3. A discovered check hits the king and something else at once.
4. Line a long piece up behind one of yours, then move the front one.
5. Two threats in one move: they can answer only one.
