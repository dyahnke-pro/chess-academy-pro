# When nothing direct works, improve your pieces

## Principle
Being better doesn't mean you can win at once. When no forcing line works, stop forcing: improve your worst piece, take away their counterplay, and let small advantages add up; attack when the position justifies it.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat. Defender's mirror: give them nothing to aim at, keep every piece guarded. Exception: when the forcing line does work, play it now.

## Deterministic check
- pictureVerdict `hard-to-finish` / `no-direct-way` / `tries-worsen` (computed), no try with net ≥ 100cp; worst piece = lowest mobility (pieceRoute).

## Phrasings
1. Being better doesn't mean you can win right away.
2. Nothing works directly, so improve your worst piece first.
3. When every active move makes it worse, play a quiet one.
4. They have nothing to do, so take your time.
5. Small advantages add up; collect them, then strike.
