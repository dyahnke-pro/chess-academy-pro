# Promotion

## Principle
A pawn on the seventh is a queen threat that bends everything; the square in front of it decides. Sometimes a knight is the right promotion (a check or a fork).

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat. Under-promotion wording: 'a new rook' only when a rook is actually chosen (card bug noted in the plan).

## Deterministic check
- Pawn on the 7th (2nd for Black) with its queening square empty or capturable; promotion legal next move; underpromotion flagged when the engine line chooses N/B/R (chess.js move.promotion).

## Phrasings
1. A pawn on the seventh is a queen threat; stop it now.
2. Their pawn queened because nothing covered the square ahead.
3. Sometimes a knight is the better promotion: it gives check.
4. Guard the queening square before anything else.
5. Your pawn is one step from a queen; clear its square.
