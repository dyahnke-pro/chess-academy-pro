# Look at checks, captures and threats first

## Principle
Every move, look at the forcing moves — checks, captures, threats — for both sides first; that's how the winning shot is found and how theirs is stopped. If a forcing line wins, play it before they fix it.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat. Exception (card row): a forcing win that looks risky is right when the line ends with your king safe.

## Deterministic check
- Habit fires when the stored read had a forcing line netting ≥ 100cp at the quiet end that the student didn't play (missed) or allowed for the opponent.

## Phrasings
1. Checks, captures and threats first, every move.
2. You had a winning shot: it started with a forcing move.
3. When a forcing line wins, play it before they fix it.
4. They have a forcing line here; stop it before your plan.
5. Look at their checks and captures before your own.
