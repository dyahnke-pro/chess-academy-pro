# Minority attack; a majority makes a passer

## Principle
A pawn majority on a wing should make a passed pawn, so push the majority, candidate first; a minority attack uses fewer pawns against the bigger group to leave it with a weak pawn.

## When it applies / exceptions
Middlegame (minority) / late middlegame–endgame (majority).

## Deterministic check
- Majority: per wing (a–c, f–h) student pawns > enemy pawns; candidate = the pawn on a file with no enemy pawn ahead (or fewer).
- Minority: student pawns < enemy pawns on a wing, the lever (b-pawn vs c-pawn in the Carlsbad, or the mirror) legal with SEE ≥ 0.

## Phrasings
1. Three pawns against two on a wing should make a passed pawn.
2. Push your majority, candidate pawn first.
3. Fewer pawns can still attack: leave their group with a weak pawn.
4. Their majority will make a passer; slow it down now.
5. In the ending, a pawn break is how you make a passer.
