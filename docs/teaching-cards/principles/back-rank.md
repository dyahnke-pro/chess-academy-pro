# Back-rank weakness

## Principle
A king boxed in on its back rank by its own pawns can be mated by one rook or queen; give it air when you have time, and never pull its last back-rank guard.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat.

## Deterministic check
- The back-rank mate test (see recovery/back-rank-weak.md): king on back rank, forward squares blocked/attacked, enemy heavy piece with a path.

## Phrasings
1. Your king had no air, and one rook check was mate.
2. Give your king an escape square when you have time.
3. Their king is boxed in by its own pawns: look at the back rank.
4. That rook was your last back-rank guard.
5. Before you move a back-rank defender, count their checks.
