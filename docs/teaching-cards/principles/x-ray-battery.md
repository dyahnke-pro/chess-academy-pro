# X-rays and batteries

## Principle
A piece behind another on the same line still counts: two pieces lined up are two attackers, and a long piece can attack or defend through an enemy piece.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat.

## Deterministic check
- Count rays through the first piece (own or enemy) to the target when SEE is computed (x-ray attackers counted after the front piece captures/moves).
- Battery: queen+bishop or rook+rook/queen on one open line toward a target or the king zone.

## Phrasings
1. Count the piece behind, not just the front one.
2. Two pieces on one line count as two attackers.
3. Their rook was defending through your piece the whole time.
4. A battery hits through: line up behind your own piece.
5. The piece at the back of that line still has a say.
