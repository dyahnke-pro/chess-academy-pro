# Combinations: forcing moves in order

## Principle
A combination is a sequence of forcing moves — checks, captures, threats — that leaves the opponent no choice; look at forcing moves first, and calculate the line to its quiet end before you commit.

## When it applies / exceptions
Either seat.

## Deterministic check
- 'Tactical sequence': the stored winning line has ≥2 consecutive forcing student moves and nets ≥ 100cp at the quiet end; the student played a non-forcing move.

## Phrasings
1. Checks, captures, threats: look at the forcing moves first.
2. Forcing moves leave them no choice; that's how combinations work.
3. Play the line to its quiet end before you trust it.
4. Every move of a combination must force a reply.
5. Their forcing line worked because each move left you one answer.
