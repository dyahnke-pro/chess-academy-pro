## The pattern

- **Most computers already teach (T).** The problem is a few huge ones that state: **pictureVerdict (660 said), readPosition (1,961, half stating), openingTheoryFacts (579), picturePlan (313), itineraryAt (122), lineCut (81), evalReading, bindingFact**. Those few produce most of the stating lines.
- **The decision teachers are T but fire rarely:** findProphylaxis 51, onlyMove 49, kneeJerk 5, countMethod 5, inBetweenMove 16.
- **Strong teachers held quiet:** theirTarget (165→2), enemyKingStuckInCenter (284→14), royalDefenderTarget (212→76), findColorComplexWeakness (437→62), planRaceClause (155→23).
- **Computed and never said:** assessPositionalEdge (876), describeStructure (648), plyFactsForMove (198) — descriptions with no why.
