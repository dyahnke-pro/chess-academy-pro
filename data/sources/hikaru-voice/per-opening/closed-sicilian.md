# Hikaru — Closed/Grand Prix Sicilian (e4 c5 Nc3 + f4) — voice corpus
Sources: https://www.chess.com/blog/RussBell/fighting-the-sicilian-with-the-grand-prix-attack
- Hikaru + Levy call the Grand Prix "Legendary". Hikaru has it prepared.
- 2.Nc3 first (stops ...d5), then f4 — strong central presence + opens lines.
- Bb5 (best-scoring: doubles Black's c-pawns via Bxc6, the c5-pawn a target) OR
  Bc4 (the original: swift O-O, Qe1-h4, f5 kingside attack). Hikaru plays both.
- Data: e4 c5 Nc3 = 2,396 games / 88%. Sharp f4-f5 attacking style.
