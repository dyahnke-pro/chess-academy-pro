## His actual system & philosophy (DATA-CONFIRMED: 5,926 games, 83%)
- 1...g6 vs almost everything — a Pirc/Modern/Hippo HYBRID. His #1 Black weapon.
- WHY (his stated reason): "we reach the same types of positions each game, giving us
  the advantage of being more familiar with the positions than our opponents." Practical
  familiarity over theory memorization.
- Gives the centre away deliberately, then challenges it; flexible, so White's early
  attacks "backfire". Very hard for White to force a draw → winning chances every game.
- Neutralizes d4 sidelines (London/Torre/Colle) + Catalan via the "Nakamura Variation".

## Black's breaks: ...e5 or ...c5 to hit White's centre; queenside expansion ...c6 + ...b5.
