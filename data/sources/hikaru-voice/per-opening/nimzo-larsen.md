## Core idea (his weapon)
- 1.b3 → 2.Bb2: fianchetto the queen's bishop onto the long diagonal, hypermodern
  control of e5/the centre. The whole game amplifies the b2-bishop's diagonal via
  move-orders, exchanges, and pawn breaks until it dominates.
- Used by Hikaru as a SURPLUS weapon in super-GM online blitz (also Carlsen, Rapport,
  Nepo, Artemiev, Jobava). Beat Ding Liren with it in 2021.
- Both positional AND tactical: slow maneuvering, then the b2-bishop + queen + rooks
  line up on central/kingside targets and tactics erupt suddenly. Flexibility is the
  point — keep options open, hard for the opponent to prepare against.

## Three main branches (data + theory)
1. vs classical centre (1...e5): allow it, undermine hypermodern — 2.Bb2 Nc6 3.e3 d5
   4.Bb5 (pin/pressure), with f4 + Nf3 hitting e5.
2. d5-square contest: 3.c4 disputes d5, English-structure transposition.
3. vs 1...d5 2...c5: a REVERSED Nimzo-Indian with an extra tempo for White.

## Pawn breaks: f4 (hits e5), e4, c4 (contests d5 English-style), d4 (vs solid setups).

## Practical/pedagogical edge (his real reason): Black rarely has experience in these
positions — colours reversed, a move down — so Hikaru out-knows them by familiarity.
