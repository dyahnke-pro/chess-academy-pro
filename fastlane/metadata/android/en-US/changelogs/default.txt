What's new in 3.5

TRAPS, WIRED INTO LEARN WITH COACH
• Ask for the traps in any opening and the coach teaches them. 344 curated trap lines across 86 openings, each one starting from a slip real players actually make, with the punishment verified by the engine.
• Every variation tile now shows the move that makes it that line, so you can spot the one you meant at a glance. Berlin 3...Nf6, Exchange 4.Bxc6, Advance 3.e5.
• Ask for a line by its move and you will get it. "The Vienna Gambit main line with Qf3" now lands on the right lesson instead of coming up empty.

LESSONS STAY ON YOUR OPENING
• Fixed a bug where the first move of a lesson could be taught with a completely different opening's material. Verified across six openings on live prod.
• Fixed beats repeating themselves, narration restarting after you switch apps, and the board snapping back to move one when you tapped Watch the middlegame.
• The middlegame play out now teaches at the same level as the opening, and waits for each line to finish before moving the pieces.

THE COACH SPEAKS YOUR LANGUAGE
• 15 languages, everywhere the coach talks. Lessons, walkthroughs, the play out, chat. Moves stay in normal notation.

A FAIRER OPPONENT ON iPHONE
• The coach was quietly playing at full engine strength on iOS no matter what rating you set. It now plays at the strength you picked.

Plus the usual fixes and polish. Pick an opening and go.
